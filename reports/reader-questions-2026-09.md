# Reader questions — 2026-09 (unpublished working note)
**Provenance:** these are the research agents' *inferences* about what LGBTQ+ people 55+ ask when considering each place. The agents ran out of search budget before reading forum threads, so **none of these were observed in Reddit or other forums**. Treat them as topics to answer with sourced facts, not as evidence of what people actually ask. A real forum-reading pass (check each site's terms first; paraphrase only; never quote or cite) is still open — see tasks/tasks-0001 notes.

## asheville
- How much has Hurricane Helene changed flood risk, insurance costs and which neighborhoods are safest to buy in?
- What do Medicare-age healthcare costs and hospital access look like in the Asheville-Hendersonville area?
- How does cost of living and home pricing compare with other retirement towns?
- How active and social is the LGBTQ+ community outside the fall Pride festival, and is it easy to meet people?

## raleigh-durham

- What is Village Hearth Cohousing and how does one join or find similar LGBTQ+ 55+ housing?
